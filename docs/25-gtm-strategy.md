## 58 · Go-To-Market Strategy

### Positioning & messaging
> **"Book anything that moves — with money that's protected."** Consumer: safe + simple. Vendor: demand + guaranteed payout. Corporate: control + compliance.

### Phased GTM

| Phase | Window | Focus | Channels | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ignite (Lagos)** | Wk 10-16 | Rides + dispatch, 50 anchor vendors, waitlist activation | referral loop (₦1k/₦1k), campus ambassadors (UNILAG/YABATECH/LASU), radio drive-time, Twitter/X + TikTok creators, mosque/church/community leads | 25k installs, 4.2 orders/user/mo |
| **Expand** | M4-9 | 9 more cities + travel + security | city-by-city playbook: local radio + market unions (NURTW engagement, tricycle/bike associations), Google/UAC performance, WhatsApp communities | CAC ≤ ₦3,400 |
| **Enterprise wedge** | M4-12 | Corporate bundles | direct sales (below), banking/FMCG/oil&gas/tech | 40 corporates by M9 |
| **Aviation & premium** | M10+ | Charter/air ambulance/exec protection | LinkedIn ABM, aviation brokers partnerships, events (Business Travel shows) | $250k/mo vertical GMV |
| **Diaspora corridors** | M22+ | UAE/UK/US pay-for-family | diaspora associations, remittance partnerships, ethnic media | 30% of corridor-city GMV |

### Launch motion per city (6-week playbook)
1. Wk-6: hire City Manager; map vendor density; regulator engagement (state transport permit).
2. Wk-4: verification events (docs day at partner venues — target 120 cars, 60 bikes, 10 logistics, 5 travel, 3 security).
3. Wk-2: driver/rider training; corporate pre-sales (10 LOIs); heat-map incentive design.
4. Wk 0: invite-code soft launch; liquidity checkpoints (match ≥80%, ETA p50 ≤ 8 min) → then marketing on.
5. Wk+2: public launch + surge of supply incentives in hotspots; weekly city P&L review.

### Pricing & promo strategy
Launch: 20% off first 2 rides; ₦1,000 referral both sides; wallet top-up bonus 2% (drives escrow adoption); vendor zero-commission first 60 days/city; loyalty from day 1 (points on all verticals).

### Brand & channels
Category education ("escrow" explainer campaigns) · safety stories (SOS testimonials) · vendor success stories. Mix: 40% performance, 30% community/field, 20% content/brand, 10% partnerships.

## 59 · Vendor Acquisition Strategy

**Target pipeline (18 mo):** 3,000 vehicles across classes, 1,500 bikes, 250 logistics/courier companies, 120 travel agencies, 60 security firms, 40 aviation/marine operators.

### Playbook per vendor segment

| Segment | Hook | Channel | Close tactic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Taxi/fleet owners | Demand + escrow-guaranteed payout + free fleet tooling | union partnerships, field team, WhatsApp groups | 60-day zero commission + on-site verification day |
| Individual drivers | instant payout, no hidden fees, safety | driver referral (₦5k), fuel-station activations, existing driver WhatsApp/Telegram groups | in-person onboarding kiosks, starter bonus after 10 trips |
| Dispatch riders | steady jobs, POD that protects them from false claims | rider associations, bike parks | earnings guarantee week 1-4 |
| Logistics cos | corporate deal flow, multi-stop optimization API | LinkedIn + industry association (NIPOST-adjacent), trade events | pilot contract via corporate demand we bring |
| Travel agencies | escrow that converts skeptical online buyers, GDS access | agency associations (NANTA), trade press | zero listing fee 6 mo, co-marketing |
| Security companies | vetted lead flow, milestone payments, ops software | industry associations, security expos, referrals | compliance concierge (we help assemble docs) |
| Aviation operators | self-serve demand channel, escrow trust | brokers, FBO partnerships, LinkedIn ABM | curated onboarding, white-glove listing |

### Field engine
City verification events weekly · "vendor success managers" own cohorts of 100 · weekly payout reliability digest (trust retention) · vendor council (advisory, quarterly) · churn alarms: acceptance drop >15% → same-week intervention.

### Verification scale
Self-serve doc upload + 48h SLA + physical verification days per city; automated CAC/TIN/BVN checks cut manual work ~60%; security providers get dedicated compliance concierge (higher stakes, higher LTV).

## 60 · Corporate Sales Strategy

**Target:** 120 accounts by M18; focus verticals: banking, FMCG, oil & gas, telecom, tech, insurance, professional services, events.

### Offer ladder

| Tier | Fit | Includes | Commercials |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team | 10-50 staff | portal, wallets, monthly billing | list take rate + ₦150k/mo |
| Business | 50-300 | + approvals, budgets, policy engine, analytics | negotiated take −1pt + ₦450k/mo |
| Enterprise | 300+ | + API, SSO, dedicated AM, custom SLAs, security retainers, aviation desk | negotiated + ₦1.5-2.5M/mo platform fee |

### Sales motion (land → expand)
1. **Land** with the pain-most service: executive transport or courier (replace broker chaos). Pilot: 2-4 weeks, 10-30 staff, success criteria (savings ≥10%, compliance 100% visibility).
2. **Expand** into security coordination + travel (attach rate goal 2.6 verticals/account).
3. **Embed**: API/SSO + policy engine = switching cost; quarterly business reviews with analytics.

### Pipeline math (M18 target)
120 accounts · avg $2.1k/mo platform+commission = $250k/mo (≈60% of M18 net revenue target from corporate mix 15% GMV).
Motion: 2 enterprise AEs + 1 CSM per 25 accounts; partner channel: travel management companies & facility managers (10% referral fee); target close rate 25% pilot→contract.

### Retention & expansion levers
Monthly invoice with WHT/VAT compliance pack · spend dashboards to CFO · SLA credits · loyalty pooling for employee benefits · annual pricing locked with volume bands.
