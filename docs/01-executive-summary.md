## 1. Executive Summary

**Africa Mobility Super App (AMSA)** is a technology marketplace that unifies ten high-demand verticals into one platform and one wallet: **Transportation, Logistics & Delivery, Travel, Aviation, Marine (future-ready), a verified Security Services Marketplace, Corporate Services, a Vendor Marketplace, Wallet & Escrow, and AI Automation.**

AMSA owns **no cars, no bikes, no aircraft, no boats, and no security companies**. Every service is delivered by verified third-party vendors. AMSA owns the demand, the trust layer, the payments rail, and the data — the highest-margin, lowest-capex position in the mobility value chain.

### The Problem

| Gap | Reality today |
|---|---|
| Fragmentation | A Lagos corporate traveller uses 5+ apps: ride-hail, courier, flight booking, a travel agent (offline), and a security contact (phone calls, no SLA) |
| Trust deficit | Premium services (chauffeur, jets, executive protection) are sold via WhatsApp/word-of-mouth with no verified vendors, no escrow, no recourse |
| Vendor lock-out | Small fleet owners, dispatch riders, and security firms cannot reach corporate demand or affordable digital tooling |
| Payment risk | Vendors demand prepayment; customers fear non-delivery. Nobody holds the middle |
| Safety | No standardized SOS, trip sharing, or verified-identity chain across services |

### The Solution

One app, one wallet, one verified vendor network across **10 verticals**, with **escrow-protected payments**, **AI matching and pricing**, and **enterprise-grade KYC/compliance** — consumer-grade UX on top, marketplace economics underneath.

### Market

- Nigeria: 220M+ people, median age ~18, smartphone penetration rising fast, ~40% urbanized; Lagos alone is 20M+ people generating the majority of national GDP.
- Africa ride-hailing and logistics TAM estimated in the **tens of billions of USD annually**; aviation and premium security are large, opaque, high-ticket markets with no dominant digital player.
- Beachhead SAM: mobility + logistics + travel in 10 launch cities ≈ **$3–5B annual spend**. Year-1 SOM target: 0.1–0.3% (≈ $4–10M GMV).

See `23-costs-financials.md` for full projections.

### Business Model (summary)

| Revenue stream | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Commission (core) | 8–20% take rate by vertical on escrow-released bookings |
| Vendor SaaS subscriptions | Free / Standard / Professional / Enterprise plans |
| Corporate contracts | Monthly billed, negotiated take rate + platform fee |
| Wallet float & FX | Interest on held balances (compliant), currency spread on travel/aviation |
| Featured listings & ads | Priority ranking, promoted vendors |
| Travel & aviation booking fees | Markup/consolidator margin on GDS inventory (Amadeus/Sabre) |
| AI & API products (Phase 3) | Fraud scoring, routing APIs for enterprises |

### Launch Plan

- **Phase 1 (MVP, Months 0–6):** Nigeria — Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Ibadan, Onitsha, Awka, Enugu, Benin City, Asaba. Verticals: Transportation, Logistics, Travel, Security Marketplace, Wallet & Escrow.
- **Phase 2 (Months 12–24):** Aviation (jets, helicopters, charter, air ambulance), Marine readiness, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa.
- **Phase 3 (Months 24–36):** UAE, UK, USA corridors (diaspora-driven demand: book rides/deliveries for family, pay from abroad).

### The Ask

**$3.5M seed** for 18 months of runway: product (60%), vendor & city operations (25%), marketing/GTM (15%). Targets: 250k app installs, 6,500 active vendors/drivers, $28M cumulative GMV, $4.1M net revenue by Month 18. Full use of funds in `23-costs-financials.md`.

### Why Now

1. **Payment rails matured** — Paystack/Flutterwave/Monnify make wallet + escrow programmable at scale.
2. **Trust economy gap** — security & premium mobility remain analog; first credible platform wins the category.
3. **Cost of AI collapsed** — fare prediction, fraud detection, multilingual support (English, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Pidgin) are now buildable by a seed-stage team.
4. **Regulatory clarity improving** — NDPR, state-level ride-hail licensing, and KYC infrastructure (NIN/BVN) enable compliant verification at onboarding speed.

## 2. Company Vision & Mission

### Vision Statement

> **To become Africa's operating system for movement, delivery, travel, and protection — and to export that trust infrastructure to the world.**

### Mission Statement

> **We connect people and businesses to verified, vetted, and insured service providers across every mode of mobility and logistics — with transparent pricing, escrow-protected payments, and intelligent automation — while creating dignified income for African vendors, drivers, and riders.**

### Core Values

| Value | What it means in product terms |
|---|---|
| **Trust is the product** | Every vendor KYC-verified; every naira escrow-protected; every trip traceable |
| **Safety without compromise** | SOS, live sharing, face verification, trusted contacts — on every vertical |
| **Vendors are partners** | Fair commissions, fast payouts, free-tier tooling, transparent scorecards |
| **Built for Africa, designed for the world** | Offline tolerance, low-bandwidth fallbacks (SMS/USSD), 5 launch languages, multi-currency from day one |
| **Data-driven honesty** | AI explains prices; metrics drive ops; no dark patterns |
| **Inclusive growth** | Rider/driver earnings programs, loyalty for everyday users, enterprise tools for SMEs |

### Strategic Pillars (3-Year)

1. **Supply-first trust network** — 10,000+ verified vendors/drivers by Month 24; verification SLA < 48h.
2. **One wallet, every service** — cross-vertical wallet spend > 35% of GMV by Month 18.
3. **Corporate wedge** — corporate transport + logistics + security bundles displace informal arrangements; 150 corporate accounts by Month 24.
4. **AI-operated marketplace** — 70% of support contacts resolved by AI; fraud loss < 0.4% of GMV.
5. **Pan-African, then global** — 4 countries by Month 24; 3 diaspora corridors by Month 36.
